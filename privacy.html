Privacy & Affiliate Disclosure

Affiliate disclosure

GolfBallDeals.co.uk earns a commission when you click through to a retailer and make a purchase. This happens through a mix of affiliate networks — including Awin, Partnerize, FlexOffers, and Amazon Associates — and auto-affiliation services (Skimlinks, and possibly Sovrn in future) that automatically convert an outbound link into an affiliate link at the point you click it, without us needing to be individually approved by every retailer first. Either way, this doesn't affect the price you pay. Deals are ranked purely by discount off RRP as verified directly against each retailer's own site — affiliate commission has no bearing on ranking or placement.

Cookies

This site loads Google Analytics and, for auto-affiliation, a Skimlinks script (and possibly Sovrn in future) that can rewrite outbound retailer links at the moment you click them. When you click through to a retailer, that retailer and/or the relevant affiliate network (Awin, Partnerize, FlexOffers, Amazon Associates, Skimlinks, Sovrn) may set a cookie on their own domain to attribute any resulting sale back to this site, in line with standard affiliate marketing practice.

What data we collect

We don't collect personal data, run user accounts, or use analytics that identify individual visitors. Standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) may be retained briefly by our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) for security and operational purposes — see GitHub's Pages data collection notice.

Prices & accuracy

Prices and stock levels are pulled from retailer feeds and refreshed roughly once a day. Always confirm the current price on the retailer's own site before buying — prices can change between our refresh and your visit.
